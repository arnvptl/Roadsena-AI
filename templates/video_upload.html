How It Works
1
Upload Video
Any dashcam .mp4 from driving on city roads.
2
Frame Extraction (every 2 s)
OpenCV extracts one frame per 2 seconds of footage. Every frame is processed — not just defect ones.
3
AI Analysis + Exact Count
YOLOv8 annotates bounding boxes on every defect. The system counts potholes per frame and estimates unique potholes from the peak frame count (so a pothole in 4 consecutive frames still counts as 1).
4
PDF with All Frames
Download a full PDF — every 2-second frame in a grid, annotated images with bounding boxes, per-frame score, and a cover summary showing unique pothole count.
A 10-min video (~300 frames) takes about 2–3 min.
The GPS you enter applies to all detections in this video.
